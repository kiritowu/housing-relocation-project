# High Lives: The Poetics and Politics of Housing Relocation in Singapore Social and Literary History

## Background

The project examines how housing relocation in Singapore was represented and debated in public discourse, drawing on historical newspaper articles from digital archives. Starting from curated spreadsheets of source articles, the codebase is structured based on the following computational research pipeline:

1. **Data Collection:** scraping historical newspaper articles and their metadata from digital archives.
2. **Dataset construction:** organising article images and metadata into a structured research dataset.
3. **Digitisation:** extracting text from scanned articles using OCR, and validating the output.
4. **Analysis:** exploratory data analysis and sentiment analysis to identify themes, patterns, and trends over time.
5. **Interpretation:** translating the findings into insights that inform the project's research direction.
